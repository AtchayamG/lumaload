## What is LumaLoad?

A person recovering from a concussion doesn't experience demand as one number. An hour in a quiet room writing is not the same as an hour in a noisy cafeteria or looking at a flickering screen. 

LumaLoad maps the **cognitive, sensory, and physical load** hidden inside an ordinary day, measuring each demand strand against a dynamic **Capacity Baseline** calculated from actual sleep, mood, fatigue, and fogginess. Using a custom SVG **Load Ribbon**, LumaLoad reveals invisible neurological pressure points before they trigger symptom spikes, providing evidence-backed, clinician-aligned schedule adjustments.

## The Safety Model

LumaLoad operates under a strict three-tier deterministic safety architecture designed for high-stakes health contexts:

1. **Deterministic Guardrails Decoupled from Generative AI**  
   Emergency danger signs (unequal pupils, worsening headache, repeated vomiting, slurred speech, seizures, numbness) bypass generative models entirely, triggering immediate emergency stop signposts and clinical escalation. High-risk activities (contact sports, driving before clearance) are permanently locked and cannot be scheduled or approved by any AI stage.
2. **Zero Server-Side Health Persistence**  
   All symptoms, schedules, and daily plans remain strictly local inside the user's browser (`localStorage`). No patient health information (PHI) is ever saved to a database, external log, or remote telemetry server.
3. **Active Evidence Verifier with Claim Purging**  
   Every AI recommendation must cite an immutable evidence chunk ID from our verified clinical evidence registry. An independent verifier stage cross-examines proposed actions against the cited text. Any recommendation containing unsubstantiated claims or hallucinatory assertions is purged before reaching the user.

## Curated Evidence Sources & Attribution

LumaLoad grounds all planning heuristics and model recommendations in six verified, peer-reviewed clinical guidelines (`src/data/evidence.json`):

1. **CDC HEADS UP to Youth Sports** — Centers for Disease Control and Prevention concussion recovery protocols, return-to-learn steps, and symptom threshold guidelines.
2. **Amsterdam 2023 Consensus Statement on Concussion in Sport** — 6th International Conference on Concussion in Sport guidelines on active recovery, sub-symptom-threshold aerobic activity, and dual-task loading.
3. **American Academy of Neurology (AAN)** — Summary of Evidence-Based Guideline for Clinicians on the Evaluation and Management of Concussion in Sports.
4. **Ontario Neurotrauma Foundation (ONF / Living Concussion Guidelines)** — Clinical Practice Guidelines for Management of Concussion / Mild Traumatic Brain Injury in Adults.
5. **National Health Service (NHS UK)** — Guidance on head injury recovery, screen management, gradual cognitive reintroduction, and red-flag escalation signs.
6. **Mayo Clinic Concussion Protocol** — Patient education on resting cognitive reserves, pacing, and sensory environment regulation.

## Medical Notice

**LumaLoad is a daily planning aid, not a medical device.** It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any medical condition, nor does it provide clinical return-to-learn, return-to-work, or return-to-play clearance. Healthcare professional instructions always take absolute precedence over any suggestion generated by this software.

## Technical Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 14+ (App Router), TypeScript (strict mode)
- **Styling:** Handcrafted CSS variables (`src/styles/tokens.css`) — zero heavy UI dependencies
- **Cartography:** Hand-rolled custom SVG `LoadRibbon` with smooth Catmull-Rom splines, unstacked multi-axis demand strands, and capacity floor breakthrough detection
- **Intelligence:** Google Gemini (cascade: `gemini-3.8-flash` → `gemini-3.5-flash` → `gemini-2.5-flash` → `gemini-2.0-flash` → `gemini-1.5-flash`) via `@google/genai` with deterministic fallback
- **Data Validation:** Zod schemas enforcing runtime contracts across all pipeline stages
- **Testing:** Vitest test suite covering deterministic safety, heuristics, capacity modeling, and evidence registry integrity
